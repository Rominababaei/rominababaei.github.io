01 · About

Curious about the space between an idea and a working method.

I am drawn to problems where intuition suggests the structure and rigorous analysis decides whether it holds.

What interests me most in a difficult problem is the structure beneath it: which information matters, which assumptions are carrying the argument, and what kind of model can explain the observed behavior. I am comfortable moving between abstraction and computation; mathematical reasoning helps me formulate the question precisely, while implementation reveals gaps that an elegant derivation can hide.

That feedback loop is what draws me to machine learning. I enjoy following a method from formulation to experiments, testing not just its performance but the logic behind it. The research I find most compelling combines conceptual depth with the discipline of building, diagnosing, and refining systems in practice.
